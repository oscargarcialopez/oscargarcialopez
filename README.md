## Featured

**[spain-vote-ev](https://github.com/oscargarcialopez/spain-vote-ev)** — What is one vote worth
in Spain's general election of 29 November 2026? A poll median with errors calibrated on
2015–2023, D'Hondt in all 52 constituencies and the investiture rules of article 99, simulated
100,000 times. Backtested on the 2019 and 2023 elections, with every assumption varied in public.

**[llm-double-auction](https://github.com/oscargarcialopez/llm-double-auction)** — Gemini agents
on both sides of a sealed-bid double auction, benchmarked against the Myerson–Satterthwaite
bound. Flash plays the equilibrium to the cent, even when the game is told as selling a used
camera lens; Flash-Lite realises 45% of the gains where 84% is possible, and a smarter rival can
exploit it. 1,240 calls, $2 of API usage.

**[rag-temario](https://github.com/oscargarcialopez/rag-temario)** — RAG over 1,280 pages
of Spanish statistical regulation, evaluated on real exam questions labelled by the examiner.
BM25 already ranks the right area first for 93% of them; embeddings add little there but
detect unanswerable questions far better (AUC 0.98 vs 0.70), and rank fusion throws that
signal away. The README also lists everything that broke.
